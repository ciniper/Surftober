Breaking-wave spin animation

Twelve frames of a breaking wave sit around the ring's outer band, running clockwise. To your eye a spinning ring is a blur. A phone video only samples it, though: when the ring turns exactly one slot per video frame, each slot shows the next frame and the wave breaks in place. A spinner slows down steadily, so it passes through that speed on its own. Flick it counter-clockwise as you face it; the other way plays the wave backwards. Film it in bright light so the shutter is fast enough not to blur.

The 12 frames, in order

Navy water and cream foam on the medal colour. Print them as flush colour changes in the top layers, not raised, so every slot weighs the same and the ring stays balanced. The smallest drops are about 0.6 mm; check them against your nozzle.
