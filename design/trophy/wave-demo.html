Breaking-wave spin animation

The frames of a wave sit in equal slots around the ring's outer band, running clockwise. To your eye a spinning ring is a blur. A phone video only samples it, though: when the ring turns exactly one slot per video frame, each slot shows the next frame and the wave plays in place. A spinner slows down steadily, so it passes through that speed on its own. Flick it counter-clockwise as you face it; the other way plays it backwards. Film it in bright light so the shutter is fast enough not to blur.
